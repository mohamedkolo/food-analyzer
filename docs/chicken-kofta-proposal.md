# PROPOSED new recipe: chicken kofta (review only)

Status: a NEW proposed recipe. It does not match, replace or recover the doctor's "كفتة دجاج مشوية" meals, and it is not applied
to any plan, library entry or client. Not wired into meal_macros pricing.

## Recipe (source limits stated)
This is a proposed household-portion formulation, not source-specified grams: only the chicken has a gram weight in the source.

Ingredient list and spices come from a dietitian-authored recipe:
Diabetes Australia, "Chicken Kofta", by Rebecca McPhee, APD (kofta part only): https://www.diabetesaustralia.com.au/recipe/chicken-kofta/
Cross-checked as a recipe type (ground chicken, onion, garlic, parsley, warm spices; 8 patties, cooked 4-5 min per side) against
The Mediterranean Dish: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/grilled-chicken-kofta/ (different amounts, not used for the numbers).

Kofta mix, raw:
- 500 g chicken mince (source gives grams) - USDA SR Legacy 171116 Chicken, ground, raw
- 1 small brown onion, finely chopped - source gives no grams; USDA household portion "small" = 70 g (170000 Onions, raw)
- 1 garlic clove - USDA portion 1 clove = 3 g (169230)
- 1 tbsp parsley - USDA portion 1 tbsp = 3.8 g (170416)
- 1 tsp paprika 2.3 g (171329); 1/4 tsp ground ginger 0.45 g (170926); 1 tsp cumin 2.1 g (170923);
  1/2 tsp coriander 0.9 g (170922); 1/4 tsp chilli flakes 0.45 g (cayenne 170932, optional)
Total raw mix 583 g. The source's own grams are only the 500 g chicken. The other amounts are USDA household portions for the
quantities the source names. Spice weights per teaspoon are from the USDA portion table, not from the recipe.

## Nutrition, per 100 g RAW mix (computed from the USDA rows above, not copied from the recipe page)
132 kcal, 15.3 g protein, 7.1 g fat, 1.9 g carbohydrate.
(For comparison, plain raw ground chicken 171116 is 143 kcal / 17.4 g protein. The library's kofta row is 250 kcal / 18 g protein per 100 g.)
The recipe page's own nutrition (437 kcal, 36.9 g protein per serving) includes a watermelon and feta salad, so it cannot be used for the kofta alone.

## What is not sourced: cooked weight
The library's items are written in grams like "كفتة دجاج مشوية 200جم", raw or cooked is UNVERIFIED (not established either way). A raw-to-cooked yield for ground chicken
patties is not in the USDA Table of Cooking Yields for Meat and Poultry, Release 2 (Sept 2014). The table has ground beef, pork and
other studies (beef patties broiled: 63% yield) but no ground chicken. So per-100 g cooked values cannot be sourced; no other meat's yield is substituted. Whether the library grams are raw or cooked is itself unverified, so this stays conditional.
Only the raw-weight basis above is sourced. For illustration only (not used anywhere): if cooked weight were 70% to 85% of raw, 100 g
cooked would be 155 to 188 kcal and 18 to 22 g protein.

## Blocker and what would settle it
A credible, source-backed COMPLETE recipe for cooked-gram portions needs one of: (a) a published cooking yield for ground chicken kofta
or patties (a measured yield or a lab nutrient analysis of cooked chicken kofta), (b) the doctor writes the portion in raw grams, or
(c) the user/doctor supplies their own kofta recipe and yield. Until one of these exists, chicken kofta stays unresolved in the original
library and is not counted in any source-matched level.
